Suppose you visit your doctor and he/she orders certain scan to be performed, you then call the provider who would handle the scan for you and they in turn contact your ‘Health Plan’ (Insurer). Depending on the type of the scan/procedure, your health plan may require a ‘Prior Authorization’. so, what is Prior Authorization (or Prior Auth)?

*Prior Auth is an utilization Management process that requires a healthcare provider to get approval from a ‘Health Plan’ before providing the service (in this case the scan) or medication.*

The rationale behind Prior Auth is to ensure that a less expensive option has been considered rather than simply opting for an expensive option. This in principle should make less expensive options available to a patient, thus lowering the overall cost.

The ability/availability of a prescribed course of treatment hinges on the approval ( or rejection) of the prior authorization by the health insurer.

**Why is Prior Auth so complex (and time taking)?**

There are multiple factors that make this process complex and time-taking, primarily:

-   There is a lot of ‘to and fro’ interaction between all participating parties (providers, payers) introducing delays in each step
-   Often the participating parties do not follow a single standard- steps are often governed by the payers
-   Rules change, necessitating changes to the process flow, sometimes also leading to confusions and re-work
-   Plans vary by payer and the Health Plans
-   Payers may require some prior auth requests to be reviewed by a medical board, this often introduces delays.

**Can automation help?**

Automation and that too early on in the overall cycle can help reduce the wastage of time by :

-   Identifying error and omissions and routing incomplete requests for additional information without needing manual review or intervention
-   Further it can help in such steps as member matching, plans and benefits determination etc.
-   Approvals (and denials) can be conveyed faster
-   Lengthy medical reviews can be avoided to the extent possible.

**How can AI (and Gen AI in particular) help?**

AI can help augment the automation process by :

-   Identifying patterns of fraud and abuse and flagging such requests early on
-   It could also help identify (and in some cases fill in) missing data (such as wrong procedure codes, ill formed addresses)
-   Based on precedence a model can help identifying the medical necessity of a procedure even suggesting alternatives
-   Speed up approval process thus increasing overall patient satisfaction.

Prior Auth process generates a huge amount of paperwork (and thus data). There is a treasure trove of information that often goes unused, which could otherwise been used to provide more accurate (and speedy) response to Prior Auth request.

Generative AI offers an advantage as it can provide ‘tailor made’ response to a specific prior auth request mapping it with multiple dimensions such as Patient, Providers, Health Plan etc.

Further, ‘prompt tuning’ can help generate newer insights thus making the response accurate and expedited.

Advent of Generative AI has ushered in a new era and generated a lot of renewed interest in automating healthcare processes. Prior Authorization would be ideal candidate that can take advantage of the promise of AI.
